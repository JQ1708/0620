Melting point / density / hardness / strength.

Cobalt has a much higher / stronger property value than potassium.

(c) When a small piece of potassium is added to cold water, the potassium floats and disappears as it reacts. Give two other observations that would be made when a small piece of potassium is added to cold water. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

The potassium melts into a ball and moves around the surface. It also fizzes and burns with a lilac flame.

(d) Cobalt reacts with dilute hydrochloric acid to make the salt cobalt(II) chloride. Bubbles of hydrogen gas are produced.

(i) Describe a test for hydrogen. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

Test: Place a lighted splint at the mouth of the test tube. Result: A 'squeaky' pop is heard.

(ii) The rate of reaction of cobalt with dilute hydrochloric acid can be made faster by heating the acid or by increasing its concentration. State one other way to make the rate of reaction faster. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

You can increase the surface area of the cobalt (e.g., use a powder) or add a catalyst.

(iii) Use collision theory to explain how heating the dilute hydrochloric acid makes the rate of reaction faster. [3]

Tap to reveal answer

Heating gives the particles more kinetic energy so they move faster. This leads to more frequent collisions. Also, more of the colliding molecules have sufficient energy (the activation energy) to react.

(e) When cobalt(II) chloride is added to water an equilibrium is established.

[CoCl4]2−(blue) + 6H2O ⇌ [Co(H2O)6]2+(pink) + 4Cl−

Tap to reveal answer

The solution becomes pink. Adding water increases the concentration of a reactant, so the equilibrium moves to the right to oppose the change.

(ii) Another student cools a blue solution containing [CoCl4]2−. The blue solution turns pink. What does this information indicate about the forward reaction? [1]

Tap to reveal answer

The forward reaction is exothermic.

(f) Another compound of cobalt is Co(OH)3. Deduce the charge on the cobalt ion in Co(OH)3. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

Since each hydroxide ion (OH) has a charge of 1−, the cobalt ion must have a charge of 3+.

Question 7 (0620/41_Summer_2019_Q3) - Making Use Of Metals, The Behavior Of Metals, The Periodic Table

Zinc and copper are elements next to each other in the Periodic Table.

(a) Zinc is obtained from zinc blende in a two-step process. In step 1, zinc blende is converted into zinc oxide. In step 2, zinc oxide is converted into zinc in a blast furnace. Outline how each of these steps are done. In your answer: give one chemical equation for each step, describe how zinc is removed from the blast furnace in step 2. [5]

Tap to reveal answer

Step 1: Zinc blende (ZnS) is roasted in air.
2ZnS + 3O2 → 2ZnO + 2SO2

Step 2: Zinc oxide is heated with coke (carbon), which acts as the reducing agent.
ZnO + C → Zn + CO

Removal of zinc: The zinc is produced as a gas at the high furnace temperature and is distilled off.

from white to blue.
