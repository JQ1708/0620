Question 27

(0620/41_Winter_2023_Q6) - Organic Chemistry, Acids And Bases, Polymers

Ethanol is manufactured by two methods:

method 1 fermentation of aqueous glucose

method 2 catalytic addition of steam to an alkene.

(a) Method 1 takes place at room temperature and pressure. State two other conditions needed in method 1. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

1. Presence of yeast.

2. Absence of air (anaerobic conditions).

(b) (i) State the typical temperature and pressure used in method 2. [2]

temperature _______________ °C

pressure _______________ kPa

Tap to reveal answer

Temperature: 300 °C

Pressure: 6000 kPa (or 60 atm)

(ii) Name the alkene used in method 2. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

The alkene is ethene.

(iii) State why the reaction in method 2 is referred to as an addition reaction. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

It is an addition reaction because there is only one product formed.

(c) The catalyst in method 2 is phosphoric acid, H₃PO₄. Dilute phosphoric acid is a weak acid which contains phosphate ions, PO₄³⁻.

(i) State what is meant by the term acid. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

An acid is a proton donor.

(ii) State the meaning of weak in the term weak acid. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

A weak acid shows partial dissociation (or ionisation) in water.

(iii) Determine the oxidation number of phosphorus in the PO₄³⁻ ion. Show your working. [2]

oxidation number = _______________

Tap to reveal answer

Working: Let the oxidation number of P be x. Oxygen is -2. The overall charge is -3. So, x + 4x(-2) = -3. x - 8 = -3. x = +5.

Oxidation number = +5

(d) Give one advantage of each method of production of ethanol. [2]

method 1 _______________

method 2 _______________

Tap to reveal answer

Method 1: Uses renewable resources.

Method 2: Has a high rate of reaction.
