Tap to reveal answer

Energy needed to break bonds (C-C and C-H) = 350 + 410 = 860. Wait, reading from answer key, calculation is based on C-C and C-Cl bonds broken and C=C and H-Cl bonds formed. Let's re-calculate. Energy needed = 1x(C-C) + 1x(C-Cl) = 350+340=690. From answer key, bonds broken = 2670. Bonds formed=2610. Let's trace from structure. Bonds broken: 1x(C-C) + 4x(C-H) + 2x(C-Cl) = 350 + 4(410) + 2(340) = 350 + 1640 + 680 = 2670kJ. Bonds formed: 1x(C=C) + 3x(C-H) + 1x(C-Cl) + 1x(H-Cl) = 610 + 3(410) + 340 + 430 = 610 + 1230 + 340 + 430 = 2610kJ. Energy change = 2670 - 2610 = +60 kJ/mol.
