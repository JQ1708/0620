(b) The equation for the complete combustion of ethanol is shown. Use the bond energies in the table to calculate the energy change, in kJ/mol, for the complete combustion of ethanol. [3]

bondbond energy in kJ/mol
C-C347
C-H413
C-O358
C=O805
O-H464
O=O498

Energy needed to break bonds. _______________ kJ

Energy released when bonds are formed. _______________ kJ

Energy change for the complete combustion of ethanol. energy change = _______________ kJ/mol

Tap to reveal answer

Energy needed to break bonds = [1(C-C) + 5(C-H) + 1(C-O) + 1(O-H)] + 3(O=O) = [347 + 5x413 + 358 + 464] + 3x498 = 3234 + 1494 = 4728 kJ.
