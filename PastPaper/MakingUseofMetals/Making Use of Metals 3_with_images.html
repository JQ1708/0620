Question 19 (0620/41_Summer_2021_Q6) - Making Use Of Metals, The Periodic Table

This question is about iron.

(a) Iron is extracted from its main ore in a blast furnace.

(i) Name the main ore of iron used in the blast furnace. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
Hematite

(ii) Name the substance that enters the blast furnace at A. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
(Hot) air

(iii) Name the substance that leaves the blast furnace at B. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
Slag / calcium silicate

(iv) Give two reasons for using coke in the blast furnace. [2]

Tap to reveal answer
1. It acts as a fuel, reacting with oxygen to release heat.
2. It acts as a reducing agent (by forming carbon monoxide) to reduce the iron ore.

(b) Another ore of iron is iron pyrites, FeS₂. Iron pyrites contains the positive ion, Fe²⁺. Deduce the formula of the negative ion in FeS₂. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
The negative ion is the disulfide ion, which has the formula S₂²⁻.

(c) Iron is a transition element. A list of properties of iron is shown.

  • Iron is a good conductor of electricity.
  • Iron forms soluble salts.
  • Iron forms coloured compounds.
  • Iron has variable oxidation states.
  • Iron acts as a catalyst.
  • Iron forms a basic oxide.

(i) Give two properties from the list in which iron differs from Group I elements. [2]

Tap to reveal answer
1. Iron forms coloured compounds.
2. Iron has variable oxidation states. (Also: Iron acts as a catalyst).

(ii) Give two properties from the list in which iron is similar to Group I elements. [2]

Tap to reveal answer
1. Iron is a good conductor of electricity.
2. Iron forms a basic oxide. (Also: Iron forms soluble salts).

(d) Steel consists mainly of iron. Iron forms rust when it reacts with water and oxygen. Magnesium blocks can be attached to the bottom of steel boats. The magnesium does not completely cover the steel.

(i) Explain how the magnesium blocks prevent iron from rusting. [2]

Tap to reveal answer
This is sacrificial protection. Magnesium is more reactive than iron, so it corrodes or loses electrons in preference to the iron.

(ii) Explain why replacing the magnesium blocks with copper blocks will not prevent the bottom of the boat from rusting. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
This is because copper is less reactive than iron and cannot act as a sacrificial anode.

(d) Name two metals from the list which are extracted by reduction of their ores using carbon. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

(e) When zinc granules are added to aqueous copper(II) sulfate, a reaction occurs. During the reaction, a red-pink solid is formed and the solution becomes colourless.

(i) Name the red-pink solid. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
Copper

(ii) Name the colourless solution. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
Zinc sulfate

(iii) Explain, in terms of particles, why the rate of this reaction increases when the temperature is increased. [3]

Tap to reveal answer
When the temperature is increased, the particles gain more kinetic energy and move faster. This leads to an increase in the frequency of collisions. Also, a greater proportion of the colliding particles will have energy equal to or greater than the activation energy, resulting in more successful collisions per unit time.

(iv) Suggest two other ways of increasing the rate of this reaction. [2]

Tap to reveal answer
1. Increase the concentration of the copper(II) sulfate solution.
2. Increase the surface area of the zinc (use a powder instead of granules).

Question 24 (0620/41_Summer_2022_Q6) - Making Use Of Metals, Acids And Bases

This question is about zinc and its compounds.

(a) Zinc is extracted from its ore which is mainly zinc sulfide, ZnS. The steps for this extraction are shown.

step 1 Zinc sulfide is converted into zinc oxide.

step 2 The zinc oxide is then reduced to zinc in a furnace. The zinc formed becomes a gas.

step 3 The zinc gas is cooled to form molten zinc.

(i) Name the ore of zinc, which is mainly zinc sulfide. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
Zinc blende

(ii) Describe how zinc sulfide is converted into zinc oxide in step 1. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
The zinc sulfide is heated strongly or roasted in air.

(iii) Name the reducing agent used in step 2. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
Carbon or carbon monoxide.

(iv) Explain why the zinc forms a gas in step 2 inside the furnace. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
The temperature in the furnace is above the boiling point of zinc.

(v) State the name of the physical change occurring when zinc gas is converted into molten zinc. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
Condensation

(b) Zinc sulfate crystals, ZnSO₄·7H₂O are hydrated. Zinc sulfate crystals are made by reacting zinc carbonate with dilute sulfuric acid. The equation for the overall process is shown.

ZnCO₃ + H₂SO₄ + 6H₂O → ZnSO₄·7H₂O + CO₂

step 1 Large pieces of solid zinc carbonate are added to dilute sulfuric acid until the zinc carbonate is in excess. This forms aqueous zinc sulfate.

step 2 The excess zinc carbonate is separated from the aqueous zinc sulfate.

step 3 The aqueous zinc sulfate is heated until a saturated solution is formed.

step 4 The saturated solution is allowed to cool and crystallise.

step 5 The crystals are removed and dried.

(i) In step 1, zinc carbonate is in excess when no more zinc carbonate dissolves. State one other observation that indicates the zinc carbonate is in excess in step 1. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
The fizzing or effervescence stops.

(ii) Name a different substance, other than zinc carbonate, that can be added to dilute sulfuric acid to produce aqueous zinc sulfate in step 1. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
Zinc / zinc oxide / zinc hydroxide.

(iii) Step 1 is repeated using powdered zinc carbonate instead of large pieces. All other conditions are kept the same. The rate of reaction increases. Give a reason why the rate of reaction increases. Explain your answer in terms of particles. [2]

Tap to reveal answer
The powdered zinc carbonate has a larger surface area. This leads to a higher frequency of collisions between the reactant particles per unit time.

(iv) Suggest what is observed when the solution is saturated in step 3. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
Crystals start to form on a glass rod when it is dipped in the solution and removed.

(v) The formula of zinc sulfate crystals is ZnSO₄·7H₂O. Give the formula of the solid formed if the crystals are heated to dryness in step 3. [1]

Tap to reveal answer
ZnSO₄
