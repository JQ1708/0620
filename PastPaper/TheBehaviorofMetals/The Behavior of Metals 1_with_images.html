(0620/43_Winter_2017_Q3) - Making Use Of Metals, The Behavior Of Metals, Acids And Bases

Iron is extracted from its ore using coke in a blast furnace.

(a) Name the ore of iron which is mainly iron(III) oxide. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

The ore of iron which is mainly iron(III) oxide is hematite.

(b) Describe the reactions occurring in the blast furnace. [6]

In your answer, include

  • two reasons for using coke in the blast furnace,
  • a chemical equation for the reduction of iron(III) oxide,
  • an explanation for using limestone in the blast furnace.
Tap to reveal answer

Coke is used in the blast furnace for two main reasons. Firstly, it reacts with oxygen from the hot air in an exothermic reaction to produce heat and increase the temperature inside the furnace. Secondly, coke acts as a reducing agent by reacting to form carbon monoxide, which then reduces the iron(III) oxide.

The chemical equation for the reduction of iron(III) oxide by carbon monoxide is: Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂

Limestone (calcium carbonate) is added to remove acidic impurities, such as silicon(IV) oxide. The heat of the furnace decomposes the limestone into calcium oxide, which then reacts with the silicon(IV) oxide to form calcium silicate, commonly known as slag.

(c) (i) Describe the bonding in iron. Include a diagram in your answer. [3]

Tap to reveal answer

Iron has metallic bonding, which consists of a giant lattice of positive ions (cations) surrounded by a sea of delocalised electrons. There is a strong electrostatic attraction between the positive ions and the mobile electrons, holding the structure together.

(ii) Use your diagram in (c)(i) to explain why iron is malleable. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

Iron is malleable because the atoms are arranged in layers. When a force is applied, these layers of positive ions can slide over one another without breaking the metallic bonds, allowing the metal to be hammered into different shapes.

(iii) Iron containing a small amount of carbon is known as steel. Explain why steel is less malleable than iron. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

Steel is an alloy where carbon atoms are mixed with iron atoms. The carbon and iron particles have different sizes, which disrupts the regular arrangement of the layers. This makes it more difficult for the layers to slide past each other, making steel harder and less malleable than pure iron.

(d) (i) When iron is added to dilute sulfuric acid, an aqueous solution of iron(II) sulfate is formed as one of the products. Write a chemical equation for the reaction. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

(ii) When iron(III) oxide is added to dilute sulfuric acid, an aqueous solution of iron(III) sulfate is formed as one of the products. Write a chemical equation for the reaction. [3]

Tap to reveal answer

Aluminium is extracted from aluminium oxide by electrolysis.

(a) Why is aluminium not extracted by heating aluminium oxide with carbon? [1]

Tap to reveal answer

Aluminium is not extracted by heating with carbon because aluminium is more reactive than carbon and therefore carbon cannot displace it from its oxide.

(b) Aluminium oxide is an ionic compound with a high melting point.

(i) Complete the dot-and-cross diagram to show the electron arrangement in one of the oxide ions present in aluminium oxide. Include the charge on the oxide ion. One of the aluminium ions is shown. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

The oxide ion should have a full outer shell with six dots and two crosses from the aluminium atoms. It should also have a charge of 2-.

(ii) The melting point of aluminium oxide is above 2000 °C. Explain why aluminium oxide has a high melting point. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

Aluminium oxide has a high melting point because there are strong electrostatic forces of attraction between the positive aluminium ions and negative oxide ions in its giant ionic lattice. A large amount of energy is required to overcome these forces.

(c) Aluminium can be extracted by electrolysis using the apparatus shown.

(i) Name the type of particle responsible for the transfer of charge in [2]

the wires,

the electrolyte.

Tap to reveal answer

In the wires, charge is transferred by electrons.

In the electrolyte, charge is transferred by ions.

(ii) Give two reasons why cryolite is used. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

1. Cryolite is used as a solvent to dissolve the aluminium oxide.

2. It lowers the operating temperature of the electrolysis from over 2000 °C to about 950 °C, which saves energy and reduces costs. It also increases conductivity.

(iii) Write the ionic half-equation for the formation of aluminium during the electrolysis. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al

(iv) Explain how carbon dioxide gas is formed at the anodes. [3]

Tap to reveal answer

Oxygen is produced at the positive anodes. The anodes are made of carbon. At the high temperatures of the cell, the hot oxygen reacts with the carbon anodes, forming carbon dioxide gas. This causes the anodes to be burned away and need regular replacement.

(d) When a piece of aluminium is placed in dilute hydrochloric acid, there is no immediate visible reaction. If the aluminium is left in the dilute hydrochloric acid for several hours, bubbles start to form. Explain why aluminium does not react immediately with dilute hydrochloric acid. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

Aluminium does not react immediately because it is coated with a thin, tough, and unreactive layer of aluminium oxide, which protects the metal from the acid.

  • give one chemical equation for each step
  • describe how zinc is removed from the blast furnace in step 2. [5]
Tap to reveal answer

Step 1: Zinc blende (zinc sulfide) is roasted in air to convert it to zinc oxide.

Chemical equation: 2ZnS + 3O₂ → 2ZnO + 2SO₂

Step 2: The zinc oxide is heated with coke (carbon) in a blast furnace, where the carbon reduces the zinc oxide to zinc metal.

Chemical equation: ZnO + C → Zn + CO OR 2ZnO + C → 2Zn + CO₂

Removal of zinc in step 2: The temperature in the blast furnace is very high, so the zinc metal is produced as a vapour. This vapour is then cooled and condensed, meaning the zinc is distilled from the furnace.
