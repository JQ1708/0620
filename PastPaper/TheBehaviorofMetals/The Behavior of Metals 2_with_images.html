(b) Aluminium is produced by the electrolysis of aluminium oxide dissolved in molten cryolite.

(i) Give two reasons why the electrolysis is done using a molten mixture of aluminium oxide and cryolite instead of molten aluminium oxide only. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

1. It lowers the operating temperature from over 2000°C to around 950°C, saving energy.

2. It dissolves the aluminium oxide and improves the conductivity of the electrolyte.

(ii) Write ionic half-equations for the reactions occurring at the electrodes. [2]

The anodes are made of carbon and they react with the oxygen produced at the high temperature of the cell. This forms carbon dioxide gas, which causes the anodes to burn away.

Tap to reveal answer

(ii) Explain how completely coating an iron object with a layer of zinc prevents rusting. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

(e) Bromine monochloride, BrCl, is made by the reaction between bromine and chlorine. The chemical equation is shown.

Br₂(g) + Cl₂(g) → 2BrCl(g)

bondbond energy in kJ/mol
Br-Br190
Cl-Cl242
Br-Cl218

Calculate the overall energy change for the reaction using bond energies.

Tap to reveal answer

Energy required to break bonds (reactants): 190 (Br-Br) + 242 (Cl-Cl) = 432 kJ

Energy released when forming bonds (products): 2 x 218 (Br-Cl) = 436 kJ

Overall energy change = Energy in - Energy out = 432 - 436 = -4 kJ/mol

(iv) The equation for the reaction is shown. 2Na(s) + 2H₂O(l) → 2NaOH(aq) + H₂(g). Calculate the concentration of NaOH(aq) formed, in g/dm³, when 0.345 g of sodium is added to 50.0 cm³ of distilled water. Assume there is no change in volume. [5]

Tap to reveal answer

Moles of Na = mass / Aᵣ = 0.345 / 23 = 0.0150 mol

From the equation, moles of NaOH = moles of Na = 0.0150 mol

Concentration in mol/dm³ = moles / volume (dm³) = 0.0150 / (50.0 / 1000) = 0.300 mol/dm³

Mᵣ of NaOH = 23 + 16 + 1 = 40

Concentration in g/dm³ = conc (mol/dm³) x Mᵣ = 0.300 x 40 = 12.0 g/dm³
