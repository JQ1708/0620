Isotopes are atoms of the same element (same number of protons) with a different number of neutrons (different mass number).

(b) A sulfide ion has the symbol shown. 34₁₆S²⁻

(i) How many neutrons are contained in this sulfide ion? [1]

(ii) How is a sulfide ion, S²⁻, formed from a sulfur atom? [1]

(iii) Which element forms an ion with a 2+ charge that has the same number of electrons as a S²⁻ ion? [1]

A S²⁻ ion has 16 + 2 = 18 electrons. The element with 20 protons that forms a 2+ ion (18 electrons) is Calcium (Ca).

(e) Sulfur dioxide reacts with aqueous sodium sulfite to produce a compound with the following composition by mass: 29.1% Na, 40.5% S and 30.4% O. Calculate the empirical formula of this compound. [3]

S: 40.5⁄32 = 1.265

(d) Ammonia reacts with oxygen as shown.

(i) Calculate the volume of oxygen at room temperature and pressure, in dm³, that reacts with 4.80 dm³ of ammonia. [3]

Tap to reveal answer

Moles of NH₃: 4.80⁄24 = 0.20 mol.

Moles of O₂: From the equation (4NH₃ + 5O₂), the ratio is 4:5. So, moles of O₂ = 0.20 × (5⁄4) = 0.25 mol.

Volume of O₂: 0.25 mol × 24 dm³/mol = 6.0 dm³.

(ii) Calculate the energy change, in kJ/mol, which occurs when one mole of NH₃ reacts. [4]

Tap to reveal answer

Energy needed to break bonds (reactants): [4 × (3 × 391)] + [5 × 498] = 4692 + 2490 = 7182 kJ.

Energy released when bonds are formed (products): [4 × 587] + [6 × (2 × 464)] = 2348 + 5568 = 7916 kJ.

Total energy change for 4 moles of NH₃: 7182 - 7916 = -734 kJ.

Energy change for one mole of NH₃: -734⁄4 = -183.5 kJ/mol.
