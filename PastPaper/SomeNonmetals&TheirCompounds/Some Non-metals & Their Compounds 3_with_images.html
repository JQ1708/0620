The pH would be between 7 and 12 (e.g., pH 9, 10, or 11).

Tap to reveal answer

(c) Limewater is a saturated solution of calcium hydroxide, Ca(OH)₂(aq).

Tap to reveal answer

Test: Add aqueous sodium hydroxide.

Observations: A white precipitate forms, which is insoluble in excess sodium hydroxide.

(d) A 25.0 cm³ sample of limewater is placed in a conical flask. The concentration of Ca(OH)₂ in the limewater is determined by titration with dilute hydrochloric acid, HCl.

(i) Name the item of apparatus used to measure the volume of acid in this titration. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

A burette.

(ii) State the type of reaction which takes place. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

Neutralisation.

(iii) As well as limewater and dilute hydrochloric acid, state what other type of substance must be added to the conical flask. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

An indicator must be added.

(iv) The equation for the reaction is shown. 20.0 cm³ of 0.0500 mol/dm³ HCl reacts with the 25.0 cm³ of Ca(OH)₂. Determine the concentration of Ca(OH)₂ in g/dm³. [5]

Tap to reveal answer

Moles of HCl: 0.0500 mol/dm³ × (20.0⁄1000) dm³ = 0.00100 mol.

Moles of Ca(OH)₂: Ratio is 1:2, so moles of Ca(OH)₂ is half the moles of HCl = 0.00100⁄2 = 0.000500 mol.

Concentration of Ca(OH)₂ (mol/dm³): 0.000500 mol⁄(25.0⁄1000) dm³ = 0.0200 mol/dm³.

Mᵣ of Ca(OH)₂: 40 + 2(16+1) = 74.

Concentration of Ca(OH)₂ (g/dm³): 0.0200 mol/dm³ × 74 g/mol = 1.48 g/dm³.

This question is about nitrogen and compounds of nitrogen.

(a) Nitrogen molecules have the formula N₂.

Nitrogen has a low melting point because there are weak forces of attraction between the molecules, which require little energy to overcome.

(iii) Explain why nitrogen does not conduct electricity. [1]

Nitrogen does not conduct electricity because it has no mobile ions or delocalised electrons to carry a charge.

Tap to reveal answer

The diagram should show a single covalent bond between the two nitrogen atoms. Each nitrogen atom should be bonded to two hydrogen atoms with single covalent bonds. Each nitrogen atom should also have one lone pair of non-bonding electrons.

Question 21

(0620/41_Winter_2022_Q3) - Some Non-metals And Their Compounds, Energy Changes And Reversible Reactions, The Speed Of A Reaction

Ammonia is made in an industrial process starting with nitrogen.

N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃

(a) Name the industrial process used to make ammonia. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

The Haber process.

(b) State the raw material from which nitrogen is obtained. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

Air.

(c) State what is meant by the symbol ⇌. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

The reaction is reversible.

(d) State the temperature and pressure used in this industrial process. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

Temperature: 450 °C

Pressure: 200 atm

(e) Name the catalyst used in this industrial process. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

Iron.

(f) The forward reaction is exothermic. State the effect, if any, on the position of the equilibrium when the following changes are made. Explain your answers. [4]

Tap to reveal answer

Temperature is reduced: The equilibrium position moves to the right-hand side. This is because the forward reaction is exothermic, and the system tries to counteract the decrease in temperature by favouring the heat-releasing reaction.

Pressure is reduced: The equilibrium position moves to the left-hand side. This is because there are more moles of gas on the left side (4 moles) than on the right side (2 moles), and the system tries to counteract the decrease in pressure by favouring the side with more gaseous moles.

(g) Explain, in terms of particles, what happens to the rate of reaction when the temperature is reduced. [3]

Tap to reveal answer

When the temperature is reduced, the reaction rate decreases. This is because particles have less kinetic energy, leading to fewer collisions per unit time. Also, a smaller proportion of these collisions have energy equal to or greater than the activation energy, resulting in fewer successful collisions.

(h) Give the formula of the compound formed when sulfuric acid reacts with ammonia. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

The formula is (NH₄)₂SO₄.

Tap to reveal answer

(d) The reaction that produces sulfur trioxide is an equilibrium. The forward reaction is exothermic.

(i) State the temperature and pressure used to make sulfur trioxide. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

Temperature: 450 °C

Pressure: 1-2 atm

(ii) Name the catalyst used. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

Vanadium(V) oxide.

(iii) Describe two features of an equilibrium. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

1. The rate of the forward reaction equals the rate of the reverse reaction.

2. The concentrations of reactants and products are constant.

(iv) State the effect, if any, on the position of equilibrium when the following changes are made. Explain your answers. [4]

Tap to reveal answer

Temperature is increased: The equilibrium shifts to the left-hand side. Since the forward reaction is exothermic, the system opposes the temperature increase by favouring the endothermic (reverse) reaction.

Pressure is increased: The equilibrium shifts to the right-hand side. This is because there are fewer moles of gas on the right side (2 moles) than on the left side (3 moles), and the system opposes the pressure increase by favouring the side with fewer gaseous moles.

(v) Explain, in terms of particles, what happens to the rate of reaction when the temperature is increased. [3]

Tap to reveal answer

Increasing the temperature increases the reaction rate. Particles gain more kinetic energy and collide more frequently. A greater proportion of these collisions have energy equal to or exceeding the activation energy, leading to more successful reactions.

(e) Name the compound formed when sulfuric acid reacts with ammonia. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

Ammonium sulfate.

(i) Calculate the volume of 0.0625 mol/dm³ dilute sulfuric acid, H₂SO₄, that completely reacts with 25.0 cm³ of 0.100 mol/dm³ potassium hydroxide, KOH, to produce aqueous potassium sulfate. [3]

Tap to reveal answer

Moles of KOH: 0.100 mol/dm³ × (25.0⁄1000) dm³ = 0.0025 mol.

Moles of H₂SO₄: Ratio is 1:2, so moles of H₂SO₄ = 0.0025⁄2 = 0.00125 mol.

Volume of H₂SO₄: Moles⁄Concentration = 0.00125 mol⁄0.0625 mol/dm³ = 0.02 dm³. In cm³, this is 20 cm³.

(ii) The experiment is repeated using the same volume and concentration of potassium hydroxide and the same concentration of dilute sulfuric acid. In this second experiment, the product is aqueous potassium hydrogensulfate, KHSO₄. Use your answer to (c)(i) and the equation to deduce the volume of H₂SO₄ required. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

In this reaction, the mole ratio of H₂SO₄ to KOH is 1:1. Therefore, 0.0025 moles of KOH will react with 0.0025 moles of H₂SO₄. Volume = 0.0025⁄0.0625 = 0.04 dm³ = 40 cm³.
