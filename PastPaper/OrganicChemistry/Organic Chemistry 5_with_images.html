(e) Ester Y has the following composition by mass: C, 58.82%; H, 9.80%; O, 31.37%. Calculate the empirical formula of ester Y. [3]

Click to reveal answer
Step 1: Divide % by Aᵣ: C = 58.82⁄12 = 4.90; H = 9.80⁄1 = 9.80; O = 31.37⁄16 = 1.96.
Step 2: Divide by smallest: C = 4.90⁄1.96 = 2.5; H = 9.80⁄1.96 = 5; O = 1.96⁄1.96 = 1.
Step 3: Multiply to get whole numbers (x2): C=5, H=10, O=2. Empirical formula = C₅H₁₀O₂.

(f) Ester Z has the empirical formula C₃H₆O and a relative molecular mass of 116. Calculate the molecular formula of ester Z. [1]

Click to reveal answer
Mass of empirical formula (C₃H₆O) = 3(12) + 6(1) + 16 = 58.
n = Molecular mass⁄Empirical formula mass = 116⁄58 = 2.
Molecular formula = (C₃H₆O)₂ = C₆H₁₂O₂.

(0620/41_Summer_2022_Q5) - Energy Changes And Reversible Reactions, Organic Chemistry, Reacting Masses And Chemical Equations

Ethanoic acid is manufactured by the reaction of methanol with carbon monoxide. An equilibrium mixture is produced. CH₃OH(g) + CO(g) ⇌ CH₃COOH(g)

(a) State two characteristics of an equilibrium. [2]

Click to reveal answer
1. The rate of the forward reaction is equal to the rate of the reverse reaction.
2. The concentrations of reactants and products are constant.

(b) The purpose of the industrial process is to produce a high yield of ethanoic acid at a high rate of reaction. The manufacture is carried out at a temperature of 300°C. The forward reaction is exothermic. Use this information to state why the manufacture is not carried out at temperatures: below 300°C, above 300°C. [2]

Click to reveal answer
Below 300°C: The rate of reaction would be too slow.
Above 300°C: The yield of ethanoic acid would be too low as the equilibrium would shift to the left (endothermic direction).

(c) Complete the table using only the words increases, decreases or no change. [3]

Adding a catalyst: rate of forward reaction increases, equilibrium yield no change.
Decreasing the pressure: rate of forward reaction decreases, equilibrium yield decreases.

Suitable catalyst: Cobalt.
Reason: It is a transition element.

(h) Ester Y has the following composition by mass: C, 48.65%; H, 8.11%; O, 43.24%. Calculate the empirical formula of ester Y. [3]

Click to reveal answer
Step 1: Divide % by Aᵣ: C = 48.65⁄12 = 4.05; H = 8.11⁄1 = 8.11; O = 43.24⁄16 = 2.70.

(d) Part of the energy profile diagram of this reaction is shown.

(i) The reaction is exothermic. Complete the energy profile diagram for this reaction. Include: the position of the products, an arrow to show the activation energy, labelled as A, an arrow to show the energy change for the reaction. [3]

Click to reveal answer
The completed diagram should show the products line below the reactants line. The activation energy (A) should be an arrow from the reactants line to the peak of the curve. The energy change should be an arrow from the reactants line down to the products line.

(ii) The chemical equation for the reaction can be represented as shown. Some bond energies are given. Use the bond energies in the table to calculate the energy change in this reaction. [3]

Click to reveal answer
