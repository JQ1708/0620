(b) One mole of ethane, C₂H₆, contains 6.02 x 10²³ molecules. Calculate how many molecules are in 15g of ethane. [1]

Click to reveal answer
Mᵣ of ethane (C₂H₆) = 2(12) + 6(1) = 30.
Moles = mass⁄Mᵣ = 15⁄30 = 0.5 moles.
Number of molecules = moles x Avogadro's constant = 0.5 x 6.02 x 10²³ = 3.01 x 10²³ molecules.

(a) An organic compound V has the following composition by mass. C, 48.65%; H, 8.11%; O, 43.24%. Calculate the empirical formula of compound V. [3]

Click to reveal answer
Step 1: Divide % by Aᵣ: C = 48.65⁄12 = 4.05; H = 8.11⁄1 = 8.11; O = 43.24⁄16 = 2.70.
Step 2: Divide by smallest: C = 4.05⁄2.70 = 1.5; H = 8.11⁄2.70 = 3; O = 2.70⁄2.70 = 1.
Step 3: Multiply to get whole numbers (x2): C=3, H=6, O=2. Empirical formula = C₃H₆O₂.
