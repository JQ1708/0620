(b) The chemical equation shows the equilibrium between dinitrogen tetroxide (N₂O₄, a colourless gas) and nitrogen dioxide (NO₂, a brown gas). N₂O₄(g) ⇌ 2NO₂(g) A mixture of dinitrogen tetroxide and nitrogen dioxide is allowed to reach equilibrium in a closed gas syringe.

(i) In chemistry, what is meant by the term equilibrium? [2]

Click to reveal answer
Equilibrium is reached in a reversible reaction when the rate of the forward reaction is equal to the rate of the backward reaction, and the concentrations of reactants and products remain constant.

(ii) If the equilibrium mixture is heated at constant pressure, a darker brown colour is seen inside the gas syringe. What does this information indicate about the decomposition of dinitrogen tetroxide? Explain your answer in terms of the position of the equilibrium. [2]

Click to reveal answer
This indicates that the forward reaction (decomposition of N₂O₄) is endothermic. When heated, the equilibrium shifts to the right to absorb the added heat, producing more of the brown NO₂ gas.

(iii) Suggest what you would see if the pressure on the equilibrium mixture were increased at constant temperature. Explain your answer in terms of the position of the equilibrium. [2]

Click to reveal answer
The mixture would become paler or less brown. Increasing the pressure causes the equilibrium to shift to the side with fewer moles of gas to reduce the pressure. The equilibrium shifts to the left, favouring the formation of colourless N₂O₄.

(iv) Use your answer to (b)(iii) to balance the chemical equation. Deduce the formula of the hydrocarbon. [2]

__CₓHᵧ(g) + __O₂(g) → __CO₂(g) + __H₂O(l)

Click to reveal answer
The ratio of volumes is the ratio of moles. So, 1CₓHᵧ(g) + 5O₂(g) → 3CO₂(g) + nH₂O(l).
From the products, we have 3 carbon atoms, so x=3.
Balancing oxygen atoms: 5 x 2 = 10 on the left. On the right, 3 x 2 = 6 in CO₂. So 10 - 6 = 4 oxygen atoms must be in H₂O. This means n=4.
From 4H₂O, we have 8 hydrogen atoms, so y=8.
Formula of the hydrocarbon = C₃H₈.
