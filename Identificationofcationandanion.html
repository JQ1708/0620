Chemist's Test Chart

A guide to identifying common ions and gases.

Precipitate Tests

Aqueous sodium hydroxide and aqueous ammonia are added to solutions to identify the metal cations below.

IonResult with Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH)Result with Ammonia (NH₃)
Ammonium (NH₄⁺)

Test: Add NaOH & warm gently.

Ammonia gas is produced (turns damp red litmus paper blue).
Aluminium (Al³⁺) White precipitate, soluble in excess (colourless solution). White precipitate, insoluble in excess.
Calcium (Ca²⁺) White precipitate, insoluble in excess. No precipitate.
Chromium (Cr³⁺) Green precipitate, soluble in excess. Green precipitate, insoluble in excess.
Copper(II) (Cu²⁺) Light blue precipitate, insoluble in excess. Light blue precipitate, soluble in excess (dark blue solution).
Iron(II) (Fe²⁺) Green precipitate, insoluble in excess. (Turns red-brown on standing). Green precipitate, insoluble in excess.
Iron(III) (Fe³⁺) Red-brown precipitate, insoluble in excess. Red-brown precipitate, insoluble in excess.
Zinc (Zn²⁺) White precipitate, soluble in excess (colourless solution). White precipitate, soluble in excess (colourless solution).

Important Note: When testing for halide (Cl⁻, Br⁻, I⁻) or sulfate (SO₄²⁻) ions, you must add dilute nitric acid first to remove any carbonate ions, which would give a false positive precipitate.

IonTestResult
Carbonate (CO₃²⁻) Add dilute acid, then bubble gas through limewater. Effervescence. Limewater turns cloudy.
Chloride (Cl⁻) Add dilute nitric acid, then silver nitrate solution. White precipitate forms.
Bromide (Br⁻) Add dilute nitric acid, then silver nitrate solution. Cream precipitate forms.
Iodide (I⁻) Add dilute nitric acid, then silver nitrate solution. Yellow precipitate forms.
Nitrate (NO₃⁻) Add sodium hydroxide solution, then aluminium foil and warm gently. Ammonia gas is produced (turns damp red litmus paper blue).
Sulfate (SO₄²⁻) Add dilute nitric acid, then barium nitrate solution. White precipitate forms.
Sulfite (SO₃²⁻) Add dilute acid, warm gently, then test gas with acidified potassium manganate(VII). Acidified potassium manganate(VII) turns from purple to colourless.
GasTestResult
Ammonia (NH₃) Test with damp red litmus paper. Litmus paper turns blue.
Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) Bubble gas through limewater. Limewater turns cloudy.
Chlorine (Cl₂) Test with damp litmus paper. Litmus paper is bleached (turns white).
Hydrogen (H₂) Place a lit splint into a test tube of the gas. A 'squeaky pop' sound is heard.
Oxygen (O₂) Place a glowing splint into a test tube of the gas. The glowing splint relights.
Sulfur Dioxide (SO₂) Bubble gas through acidified potassium manganate(VII) solution. Solution turns from purple to colourless.
