1. Air

Composition of Air

  • The present composition of gases in the atmosphere has not changed much in 200 million years.
  • About four-fifths of the air is nitrogen and one-fifth is oxygen.
  • The remaining gases include carbon dioxide, water vapour and trace quantities of the noble gases.

The two main gases in the air are nitrogen and oxygen.

Pollutants: Carbon Oxides

Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)

Source:

  • Complete combustion of carbon-containing fuels (e.g., fossil fuels).

Adverse effect:

  • Increases global warming & climate change.

Carbon Monoxide (CO)

Source:

  • Incomplete combustion of carbon-containing fuels.

Adverse effects:

  • It is a toxic / poisonous gas.
  • Reduces the capacity of blood to carry oxygen.

Pollutants Causing Acid Rain

Sulfur Dioxide (SO₂)

Source:

  • Combustion of fossil fuels containing sulfur impurities.

Oxides of Nitrogen (NOₓ)

Source:

  • High temp/pressure in car engines reacts N₂ and O₂ from the air.

Adverse Effects (Both):

  • Dissolve in clouds to form acid rain.
  • Acid rain corrodes buildings, damages aquatic life and trees.

2. Effects of Greenhouse Gases

The Greenhouse Effect

  • Sun emits shortwave radiation (UV/visible).
  • Earth absorbs this and warms up.
  • Warm Earth radiates longwave radiation (infrared).
  • Greenhouse gases (CO₂, CH₄) absorb this longwave radiation, trapping it.
  • This re-radiated energy keeps the Earth warm.

Key Greenhouse Gases & Consequences

Key Greenhouse Gases

Carbon Dioxide (CO₂):

  • Main source: combustion of fossil fuels.

Methane (CH₄):

  • Sources: fossil fuel extraction, landfills, agriculture.

Consequences:

  • Increased gases lead to climate change.
  • Effects: rising temperatures, melting ice caps, rising sea levels, extreme weather.

3. Reducing Environmental Issues

Acid Rain: Formation & Reduction

Formation

SO₂ and NOₓ mix with water vapour in clouds, forming sulfuric and nitric acids which fall as acid rain.

Reduction

To reduce SO₂:

  • Use low-sulfur fuels.
  • Use flue gas desulfurisation (reacting SO₂ with a base like CaCO₃).

To reduce NOₓ:

  • Use catalytic converters in cars.

Catalytic Converters

  • Function: Reduce pollutants from car exhausts.
  • Structure: A honeycomb structure coated with catalysts (Pt, Rh, Pd) for large surface area.
  • Reactions: Harmful gases are converted to less harmful ones.

    • 2NO + 2CO → N₂ + 2CO₂

4. Photosynthesis

Process & Equation

The Process

  • An endothermic reaction where energy from light is absorbed by chlorophyll.
  • Reactants: Carbon dioxide & water.
  • Products: Glucose & oxygen.
CARBON DIOXIDE + WATER → GLUCOSE + OXYGEN

Symbol Equation (Extended)

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
